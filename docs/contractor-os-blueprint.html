The 7-Table Relational Operating Schema
01_Dim_Jobs Project Anchor
Master Project & Margin Register
Universal thread linking every downstream quote, PO, labor timesheet, and equipment warranty back to the contract baseline.
Job_ID • Client • Scope • Contract_Value • Est_Budget • Live_Margin%
02_Dim_Takeoff_BOM Estimating WBS
Scope of Work & Bill of Materials
Every part, pipe stick, unit, or valve estimated from drawings logged with phase WBS and baseline budget unit price.
BOM_Line_ID • Phase_WBS • Part_Number • Req_Qty • Baseline_Est
03_Fact_3Way_Quotes Margin Protection
3-Way Distributor Bidding Matrix
Compares 3 competitive supply houses side-by-side on Price, Lead Time (Days), and Payment Terms (e.g. 2% 10 Net 30).
Quote_ID • Dist_A • Dist_B • Dist_C • Winning_Vendor • Margin_Savings
04_Fact_Purchase_Orders Audit Trail
PO Commitment & Versioned Baseline
Immutable audit record tracking Original PO vs Change Orders. Prevents field personnel from silently wiping out variance history.
PO_Number • Original_Baseline • PO_Change_Amt • Revised_Committed • Reason
05_Fact_Receiving_&_Credits Cash Recovery
Damaged Shipments & Vendor Credit Memos
Verifies ordered vs good vs damaged quantities on jobsite flatbed delivery. Reconciles vendor credits & restocking fees.
Receipt_ID • Good_Qty • Damaged_Qty • Credit_Memo_No • Net_Credit
06_Fact_Job_Costing Variance Engine
Labor, Material & Sub Job Cost Variance
Real-time comparison of Estimated Budget vs Committed POs vs Invoiced Actuals across all 4 cost categories.
Cost_Entry_ID • Cost_Code • Category • Committed • Invoiced • Variance
07_Dim_Equipment_Master Recurring Revenue
Installed Asset Register & Automated PM Service Intervals
Turns project closeout data into recurring service revenue. Logs unit serial numbers, model specs, 5-year manufacturer warranties, and auto-calculates Next_Service_Due dates (Quarterly, Semi-Annual, Annual).
Equipment_Asset_ID • Client • Equipment_Type • Serial_No • Warranty_End • PM_Interval • Next_Service_Due
The 3 Operational Guardrails
Guardrail 01
3-Way Bidding Discipline
Forces side-by-side comparison between distributors before issuing a PO, saving 3% to 6% on equipment procurement.
Guardrail 02
Locked Committed Costs
Captures the liability the second a PO is signed—not 30 days later when the accounting bill arrives.
Guardrail 03
Credit Memo Enforcement
Tracks short deliveries and transit damage directly back to the vendor so you never pay for materials you didn’t receive.
